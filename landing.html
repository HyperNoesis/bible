How it works

Three steps to your first recording

Genesis explains camera and microphone access before you start. Optional auto-start is off by default and requires a separate opt-in.

  1. 01

    Pick a room

    Choose a studio from the library, or use your own picture. Each room shows a real preview first.

  2. 02

    Find your frame

    Start your camera, check the light and your microphone, and step in.

  3. 03

    Record or go live

    Record with a safety copy saved as you go. Going live is in beta.

    Trust

    Privacy and honest limits

    Your picture stays with you

    • Your camera picture is processed on your device.
    • Recordings are saved on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to go live.
    • You can use Studio as a guest with no account. Guest work stays on this device.

    Read the privacy policy and the terms.

    What it is not

    • The background is a picture, so it looks best with the camera held still.
    • Older phones may need effects turned down. The built-in speed test tells you.
    • A phone browser cannot act as a camera for Zoom or Meet. Use Genesis to record or go live, or use OBS on a computer.
    • Going live needs a free relay set up once. We walk you through it.
